Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what information the nest.mn website collects, what it is used for, and who it is shared with.

Who runs this website

This website is run by VE-ST Holdings Mongolia LLC (Nest Group), Baga Toiruu Street, Building 46, 1st Khoroo, Chingeltei District, Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia. For questions about this policy, email info@nest.mn or call (+976) 7707-6977.

What you send us through the contact form

When you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, phone number (if you give one) and your message. We use them to reply to your enquiry. The form is delivered to our email inbox by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service.

To block automated spam, the form uses hCaptcha. To check that a person is sending the form, hCaptcha processes technical information such as your IP address and details about your browser and device.

What is stored on your device

The website remembers the language you choose by saving it in your browser's local storage (as “nest-lang”), so pages open in that language next time. This stays on your device. The website itself does not set cookies, but some of the services below may set their own.

Services the website uses

When a page loads, your browser connects to the services below. As with any web request, this sends them technical information such as your IP address and browser type.

  • Cloudflare: delivers and protects the website
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: counts page views and measures how fast pages load. Cloudflare says it does this without cookies and without tracking individual visitors.
  • Google Fonts: the website's typefaces
  • Google Maps: the map on the Contact page
  • Sanity: news articles and their images
  • moneyconvert.net: exchange rates on the Investment page
  • Web3Forms and hCaptcha: the contact form (see above)

What you type into the contact form goes only to Web3Forms. Each service handles information under its own privacy policy.

Links to other websites

Our YouTube, Facebook and Instagram links take you to those services, where their own privacy policies apply.

Your requests

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email info@nest.mn.

Changes to this policy

If the way this website handles information changes, we will update this page. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
