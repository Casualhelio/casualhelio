About Nest Group

NEST Group is a multi-sector group company that has been operating since 2018 across investment, investment advisory, financial management, real estate, non-banking financial services, tourism, food service, and labor placement.

With a professional team, we ensure synergy across our subsidiaries — delivering comprehensive solutions and real value to clients and investors, and building an environment where we grow sustainably together.

Our activities go beyond service delivery: we open new opportunities and spread new experiences and culture. We uphold international standards and place quality, trust, and a customer-centric approach at the heart of everything we do.

Under the slogan "Towards a more amazing tomorrow," we aim to make a tangible contribution to social and economic development and create positive impact.

The word 'NEST' symbolizes a bird's nest made of twigs — representing the opportunity to reach the next new level of life.

A Decade of Purposeful Growth

  1. 2016

    REAL ESTATE

    NEST PROPERTY SERVICE LLC Launched

    Entered the real estate market with its first 7 managed properties, growing to 120+ properties by 2025 and setting new standards in property management.

  2. 2018

    FOUNDED

    VE-ST Holdings Mongolia LLC Established

    The holding company was founded with a vision to build a diversified investment portfolio and foster growth opportunities across key sectors.

  3. 2018–2024

    FINANCE

    NEST MIRAIS NBFI LLC Established

    Launched our non-banking financial institution, growing the portfolio from 1.6B to 16.4B MNT by 2024 with competitive JPY & MNT investment rates.

  4. 2024

    ISO 9001 & ISO 27001

    Quality Management & Information Security

    We have successfully implemented the ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System across our subsidiaries, fostering a culture of continuous improvement at all levels.

    We have also implemented ISO 27001:2022 Information Security Management System, ensuring client data confidentiality, integrity, and accessibility at international standards.

  5. CSR

    Social Responsibility & Sustainable Development

    NEST Group implements social responsibility as an integral part of our organizational values and culture, annually organizing donation campaigns.

    We support environmentally friendly practices, waste reduction, and provide real support to target social groups.

  6. HR

    Human Resources & Organizational Culture

    We view our employees as the organization's most valuable capital, implementing policies that sustainably support their knowledge, skills, and personal development.

    We regularly organize language and professional training, building a capable, internationally competitive team.

  7. TODAY

    Expanding Our Legacy

    Nest Group now operates three subsidiaries and continues to grow its portfolio and deepen its regional impact.
