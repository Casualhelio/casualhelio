NEST Group is a multi-sector group company that has been operating since 2018 across investment, investment advisory, financial management, real estate, non-banking financial services, tourism, food service, and labor placement.

With a professional team, we ensure synergy across our subsidiaries, delivering comprehensive solutions and real value to clients and investors, and building an environment where we grow sustainably together.

Our activities go beyond service delivery: we open new opportunities and spread new experiences and culture. We uphold international standards and place quality, trust, and a customer-centric approach at the heart of everything we do.

Under the slogan "Towards a more amazing tomorrow," we aim to make a tangible contribution to social and economic development and create positive impact.

"NEST" creates a bird's nest, crafted from branches, it represents the potential for the new phase of a happiest life, and symbolized always ready to return at any time.

A Decade of Purposeful Growth

  1. 2016

    REAL ESTATE

    NEST PROPERTY SERVICE LLC Launched

    Entered the real estate market with its first 7 managed properties, growing to 120+ properties by 2025 and setting new standards in property management.

  2. 2018

    FOUNDED

    VE-ST Holdings Mongolia LLC Established

    The holding company was founded with a vision to build a diversified investment portfolio and foster growth opportunities across key sectors.

  3. 2018-2024

    FINANCE

    NEST MIRAIS NBFI LLC Established

    Launched our non-banking financial institution, growing the portfolio from 1.6B to 16.4B MNT by 2024 with competitive JPY & MNT investment rates.

  4. 2024

    ISO 9001 & ISO 27001

    Quality Management & Information Security

    We have successfully implemented the ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System across our subsidiaries, fostering a culture of continuous improvement at all levels.

    For us, quality management is not a one-off activity but an integral part of daily work: a system built on every employee's participation and responsibility. We proudly received our standard certification as an implementing organization on December 15, 2023.

    We have also implemented the ISO 27001:2022 Information Security Management System, ensuring client data confidentiality, integrity and availability at international standards and managing risk systematically. Under this policy, we continuously improve data protection, access control, backups, business continuity, and our employees' knowledge and culture.

  5. CSR

    Social Responsibility & Sustainable Development

    NEST Group implements social responsibility as an integral part of our organizational values and culture, annually organizing donation campaigns.

    We support environmentally friendly practices and waste reduction, and provide real support to target social groups. As part of this initiative, our team collects reusable clothes, children's toys, books and bags and delivers them to people in need, creating a positive impact on society.

    We are developing our work for the good of society not as one-off events but as a sustainable initiative carried out every year, and aim to create value for society together as a team.

  6. HR

    Human Resources & Organizational Culture

    We view our employees as the organization's most valuable capital, implementing policies that sustainably support their knowledge, skills, and personal development.

    We regularly organize language and professional training, building a capable, internationally competitive team for the future. We also create a flexible workplace that supports work-life balance, and carry out policies that support mothers with young children and single mothers, putting our social responsibility into practice.

    Our team nurtures a culture based on mutual trust, respect and shared growth, laying the foundation for the organization's long-term success.

  7. TODAY

    Expanding Our Legacy

    Nest Group now operates three subsidiaries and continues to grow its portfolio and deepen its regional impact.

Company Responsibility

We, in the context of human resources policy and social responsibility. The value of our company lies in our employees and customers. To support this, we have implemented numerous measures focused our employees' well-being and growth. These include: regular annual health checkups, additional food and transportation allowances, child allowances, special encouragement and support for women workers and mothers with young children.

Society strives to highlight women's leadership and skills in a more positive way. In this sense, we are creating opportunities for mothers to work efficiently through: child allowance policies, remote work opportunities, kindergarten solutions.

VE-ST Holdings Mongolia LLC, we are committed to fostering the self-development and advanced education of our employees through regular language and vocational training. We believe that knowledge is essential for keeping pace with the development in an ever-evolving society. In this sense, we provide every employee with the opportunity to participate in our professional development training program, which includes a 3-month internship with wage, at our headquarters in Japan. As we look to the future, we remain dedicated to build a skilled and educated workforce that will make meaningful contributions to the development of our society.

  • For Financial Assistance

    We are collaborating with national banks to implement high-interest savings accounts, home loans, and special low-interest loans for employees in need of financial assistance. Additionally, through our non-bank financial institution, VE-ST Mirais, we provide loan financing for housing, automobiles and consumer needs on a cost-effective basis.

  • Health

    The value of NEST group is our employees and customers. We prioritize the health and well-being of our employees by participating in annual preventive health screenings and tests. We are constantly supporting our employees to take extra care of their own health and prevent illness.
