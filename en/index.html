The Potential of Ultra-Central Real Estate

Ulaanbaatar is home to around half of Mongolia's population, and many apartments are located in the city center. However, because urban planning has been slow, it is still possible to buy real estate at prices relatively below its potential market value.

Projects to redevelop and renew socialist-era apartment buildings are also in their early stages. This is a sign that a new era of urban development is beginning, and a strong possibility that property values will rise sharply in the near future.

Expanding Inbound Tourism

With the opening of Chinggis Khaan International Airport, built with concessional loans and investment from Japan, Mongolia's air transport capacity has grown sharply, making it possible to receive up to 3 million passengers a year. The number of tourists visiting Mongolia has grown steadily in recent years, reaching about 650,000 in 2023 and 700,000 in 2024, with a target of welcoming 1 million tourists in the coming years.

While this growing flow of foreign visitors is rapidly expanding the tourism sector, the supply of hotels and accommodation remains relatively limited. This is a real opportunity for high-return investment in this sector.

High Value-Added Apartments

As Mongolia's economy grows and urbanization accelerates, the number of middle- and high-income citizens is rising, and demand is surging for more comfortable, smartly organized apartments with modern design. Apartments that are more livable and well-planned lease for 20-50% higher rent than ordinary units and enjoy steady demand.

As a result of our renovated apartment projects:
