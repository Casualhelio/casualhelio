Our Products

NEST Mirais NBFI provides secured loans with flexible rates, resolved same-day. Trusted, risk-free, reliable service since 2018 — serving 2,500+ clients. We prioritize our customers and work every day to provide fast and efficient service.

01 / 04
  1. 01

    Real Estate Collateral Loan

    Fast real estate secured loans — apartments, commercial spaces, parking — resolved quickly to meet your financial needs. Unique advantage: we accept older 40k–50k district apartments as collateral, which other banks typically reject.

  2. 02

    Car Collateral Loan

    Car collateral loans up to 60 months at 2.3–3.2% interest, allowing you to keep driving your vehicle. We evaluate your car at up to 50% of market value, process same-day with no service fee and flexible interest terms.

  3. 03

    Car Leasing (Japan & USA Imports)

    We partner with SABO DEALS LLC (12 years experience importing US market vehicles) to offer order-based purchase services. We also import vehicles from Japan. Pay 30% upfront and finance the rest through our leasing terms. Choose from quality vehicles at favorable prices.

  4. 04

    Mobile Number Collateral Loan

    Loans secured by premium mobile numbers (9911, 9909, 8811 prefix) — up to 5 years, up to 60% of market value, no service fee, same-day approval. Monthly interest 2.5–2.9%. Returning customers receive discounted rates.
