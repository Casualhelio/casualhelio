An ultra-central strategy that never misses the latest information and opportunities.

Ulaanbaatar is a city located in the central region of Mongolia. With an area of 47,400 km², it is roughly the size of Tokyo and Kanagawa prefectures combined. However, roughly half of the total population is concentrated in this city, which accounts for just 0.3% of the country's total land area. The city is long from east to west and short from north to south, centered around the Government Building (Parliament Building) and Sukhbaatar Square, along the main street, Enkhtaivan Road. Within a radius of about 1 km from the government buildings, there are many public facilities, including government ministries and offices, the central post office, the headquarters of major banks, and embassies of various countries.

There are also many cultural facilities, such as national universities, theaters, and museums, making it the administrative, economic, and cultural center of the country. We chose this corner of the city center as our base. The area is located in the financial and government districts, with the opposite of the Bank of Mongolia and TDB Bank nearby. It is also bustling with people, shopping malls, commercial facilities, guest houses, restaurants, and more. Backed by a location where we can catch the latest information and trends first and respond to a wide range of business opportunities, NEST proposes the best strategy for Mongolia.

About Taxes on Investments in Mongolia

Mongolia20%JAPAN

Income from real estate and interest earned in Mongolia is subject to worldwide taxation and requires filing a final tax return in Japan. This income is subject to a 20% withholding tax for non-residents. Taxes withheld and paid in Mongolia can be applied as a foreign tax credit in Japan. However, these credits are subject to specific requirements and limitations under Japanese tax law. Please consult your tax advisor for detailed information.
